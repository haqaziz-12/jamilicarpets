Meet the Weavers

The Hands Behind Every Knot

Every Jamili carpet is woven by skilled Afghan artisans who have inherited their craft through generations. These are the people whose patience, skill, and dedication make each carpet a masterpiece.

Artisan Photo

Mohammad Yousuf

Master Weaver · 35 years experience

Specialty: Khal Mohammadi & Classic patterns

Mohammad began weaving at the age of 12, learning from his father. Three decades later, he is one of our most respected master weavers, known for his precise knot work and deep understanding of traditional Afghan patterns.

Artisan Photo

Fatima Rahimi

Senior Weaver · 20 years experience

Specialty: Mamluk & Geometric designs

Fatima specializes in the intricate geometric medallions of Mamluk carpets. Her attention to symmetry and color harmony has made her one of our most sought-after weavers for custom commissions.

Artisan Photo

Abdul Rahim

Master Weaver · 28 years experience

Specialty: Kilim & Contemporary

Abdul Rahim is our foremost Kilim weaver, mastering the flat-weave technique that requires exceptional tension control. He also leads our Contemporary collection, blending traditional methods with modern aesthetics.

Artisan Photo

Zainab Hashimi

Senior Weaver · 15 years experience

Specialty: Natural Dyeing & Fine knots

Zainab oversees our natural dyeing process, preparing plant-based colors using centuries-old recipes. She also weaves our finest pieces, with knot counts exceeding 250 KPSI.

Artisan Photo

Haji Noor Ahmad

Master Dyer · 40 years experience

Specialty: Natural Dye Master

Haji Noor has been preparing natural dyes since 1980 — the year Jamili Carpets was founded. His knowledge of plant sources, mordants, and dye recipes is unmatched, producing the rich, lasting colors that define our carpets.

Artisan Photo

Nasrin Karimi

Weaver · 10 years experience

Specialty: Geometric & Tribal patterns

Nasrin represents the next generation of Afghan weavers. Trained by our master artisans, she brings fresh energy to traditional geometric patterns while maintaining the exacting standards of Jamili Carpets.

A Day at the Loom

Life in the Workshop

Our artisans work in a collaborative, respectful environment — preserving a craft that has defined Afghan culture for over two thousand years. Each weaver works at their own pace, ensuring every knot receives the care it deserves.

Morning Setup

Morning

The day begins with preparing the loom, checking wool supplies, and reviewing the pattern chart. Artisans settle into a rhythm that will continue for hours.

Weaving

Midday

Knot by knot, the carpet grows. An experienced weaver can tie 10,000+ knots per day. The workshop fills with the quiet, meditative sound of the comb beating the weft.

Finishing

Evening

As the day ends, completed sections are inspected. Wool is stored properly, looms are covered, and the carpet rests — ready for another day of weaving tomorrow.
