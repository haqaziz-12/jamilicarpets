How It Works

Your Carpet, Your Vision

Every custom order begins with your vision. Tell us what you want — size, colors, pattern, pile height — and our master artisans will bring it to life. The process typically takes 3–6 months depending on complexity and size.

01

Share Your Vision

Fill out the custom order form below with your preferred collection, size, colors, pattern, and pile height.

02

Consultation

We review your request and contact you to discuss details, provide mockups, and confirm the design.

03

Crafting

Our master artisans begin hand-weaving your carpet. We send you progress photos along the way.

04

Delivery

Once complete, your carpet is washed, inspected, and shipped to your door — anywhere in the world.
