Our Story

A Legacy Woven in Kabul

Founded in 1980 with just two looms, Jamili Carpets has grown into one of Afghanistan's most respected carpet manufacturers. What began as a small family workshop in the heart of Kabul has become a name trusted by collectors, designers, and homeowners across more than 30 countries.

For over four decades, we have remained steadfast in our commitment to the ancient art of hand-weaving. Every carpet that leaves our workshop carries the soul of Afghan tradition — the patience of our artisans, the richness of natural dyes, and the durability of premium Ghazni wool.

We don't just make carpets. We preserve a 2,000-year-old craft, support artisan families, and bring pieces of Afghan heritage into homes around the world.

Craftsmanship

From Wool to Masterpiece

Every Jamili carpet passes through five meticulous stages — each performed entirely by hand.

01

Wool Selection

Premium Ghazni wool from highland sheep, prized for its durability and natural luster.

02

Natural Dyeing

Plant-based dyes from roots, bark, and flowers — following centuries-old recipes.

03

Hand Weaving

Skilled artisans weave on traditional wooden looms, tying each knot by hand.

04

Washing & Drying

Washed in natural spring water and sun-dried to reveal true, lasting colors.

05

Finishing

Edges hand-bound, surfaces trimmed and inspected — only perfect pieces leave.
